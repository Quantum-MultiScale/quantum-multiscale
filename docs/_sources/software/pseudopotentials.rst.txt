Pseudopotentials
================
Motivation
----------

All–electron Kohn–Sham (KS) orbitals oscillate rapidly near nuclei. To make
plane–wave (or real–space) calculations efficient, we **freeze the core** and
replace the true electron–ion interaction by a smoother **pseudopotential** that
reproduces the **valence** physics outside a cutoff radius :math:`r_c`.





Kohn–Sham pseudopotentials (nonlocal)
-------------------------------------

A modern KS pseudopotential has a **local** part plus an **angular–momentum
dependent nonlocal operator**,

.. math::

   \hat V_{\mathrm{PP}}
   \;=\;
   V_{\mathrm{loc}}(r)
   \;+\;
   \sum_{\ell m} \,|\beta_{\ell m}\rangle\, D_{\ell}\, \langle \beta_{\ell m}|,

where :math:`|\beta_{\ell m}\rangle` are localized projectors (Kleinman–Bylander
form) and :math:`D_{\ell}` are channel strengths. This nonlocality lets KS PPs
treat different :math:`\ell` channels (s, p, d, …) differently—crucial for good
transferability.

**Norm-conserving condition (NCPP).** For each channel :math:`\ell`, the
pseudo–radial function :math:`\phi_{\ell}^{\mathrm{PS}}` matches the
all–electron solution :math:`\phi_{\ell}^{\mathrm{AE}}` **outside** :math:`r_c`
and conserves charge **inside** :math:`r_c`:

.. math::

   \int_0^{r_c} r^2 \, \left|\phi_{\ell}^{\mathrm{PS}}(r)\right|^2 \, dr
   \;=\;
   \int_0^{r_c} r^2 \, \left|\phi_{\ell}^{\mathrm{AE}}(r)\right|^2 \, dr,

with matching **eigenvalues** and **logarithmic derivatives** at :math:`r_c`.
That is the “**conserve**” note in your sketch.

Nonlinear core correction (NLCC)
--------------------------------

Exchange–correlation (XC) is **nonlinear** in the density. When the **valence**
density overlaps the **core** region, evaluating
:math:`E_{\mathrm{xc}}[\rho_v]` alone misses XC contributions. The **NLCC**
fix is to add a frozen core density :math:`\rho_c(r)` in XC:

.. math:: E_{\mathrm{xc}}[\rho_v] \;\to\; E_{\mathrm{xc}}[\rho_v + \rho_c].

This often improves thermochemistry, magnetism, and charge transfer for elements
with appreciable core–valence overlap (e.g., Na, Si, transition metals).

Why KS PPs are **not** directly usable in OF-DFT
------------------------------------------------

Orbital-free DFT (OF-DFT) minimizes the total energy **without Kohn–Sham
orbitals**; the only variable is the **electron density** :math:`\rho(\mathbf r)`.
Standard KS PPs are **nonlocal and orbital-dependent** (they act via
projectors on the **orbitals**), so in OF-DFT there is nothing to project.
Hence, you need a **purely local ionic potential** :math:`V_{\mathrm{ion}}(r)`
that only acts on the **density**.

Local pseudopotentials (LPP) for OF-DFT
---------------------------------------

A **local pseudopotential** is a spherically symmetric potential
:math:`V_{\mathrm{LPP}}(r)` such that the OF-DFT energy functional

.. math::

   E[\rho] = T_s^{\mathrm{OF}}[\rho] + E_{\mathrm{H}}[\rho] + E_{\mathrm{xc}}[\rho]
             + \int \rho(\mathbf r)\, V_{\mathrm{LPP}}(r)\, d\mathbf r
             + E_{\mathrm{ion\!-\!ion}},

reproduces **reference KS** forces/energies as closely as possible.

Common routes to build LPPs:

1. **Local-channel extraction.** Use one channel of an NCPP
   (often :math:`\ell=0`) as :math:`V_{\mathrm{LPP}}(r)`. Fast, but sometimes
   less transferable.

2. **Model analytic forms** fit to KS data (scattering phase shifts, atomization
   energies, bulk lattice constants). Examples include Goodwin–Needs–Heine,
   Heine–Abarenkov, error-function–screened Coulomb plus Gaussians:

   .. math::

      V_{\mathrm{LPP}}(r) \approx
      -\,\frac{Z_{\mathrm{eff}}}{r}\,\mathrm{erf}\!\left(\frac{r}{\sqrt{2}\sigma}\right)
      + \sum_{i} A_i \exp\!\left[-\left(\frac{r}{b_i}\right)^2\right].

3. **Force matching/optimization.** Fit :math:`V_{\mathrm{LPP}}` so OF-DFT
   reproduces KS **forces** and **equation of state** for test clusters/bulk.

4. **Density-derived LPP.** Derive an effective local potential that yields the
   KS valence density for a reference configuration.

Practical checklist for a good LPP
----------------------------------

- Choose **reference states** (isolated atom, small cluster, and one/bulk
  structure).
- Fit :math:`V_{\mathrm{LPP}}(r)` to match KS targets (eigenvalues,
  scattering/phase shifts, forces, lattice constant, bulk modulus).
- Validate on **transfer** tests different from the fit set (defects,
  surfaces, different coordinations).
- If core–valence overlap is relevant, consider **NLCC** by including a
  spherically averaged core density in XC (still local in OF-DFT).


EAC (BLPS and goLPS)
--------------------
Bulk local pseudopotentials (BLPSs) and globally-optimized local pseudopotentials (goLPSs) are generated by `Carter group <https://carter.princeton.edu/>`_

OEPP
----
Optimized effective pseudopotentials (OEPPs) are generated by `ATLAS developers group <http://atlas-ch.cn/>`_

NLPP
----
Pseudos by Valeria Rios Vargas and Ezekiel Oyeniyi et al. Paper to be submitted.

DFT-MUEC
--------
The transferable pseudopotentials are developed for H, Li, and Al in WDM conditions by `Density Functional Theory for Matter under Extreme Conditions <http://www.qtp.ufl.edu/>`_
